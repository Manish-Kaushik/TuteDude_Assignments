About

A small city library with room for big ideas.

Greenwood Public Library is a welcoming place to discover new ideas, spend time with family, or simply find a quiet chair. Our collection keeps growing while our focus stays simple: make learning accessible.

From story time to study sessions, local history to digital resources, the library supports people at every stage of life. Membership is free and our public programs are open to all.
